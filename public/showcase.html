BC Smart Ecosystem in detail

The full story behind the project: the problem we are tackling, how BC Ways and BC Eats work, and who they are for.

The project

What BC Smart Ecosystem is

BC Smart Ecosystem, also called BC Smart Lifestyle in our proposal, is a single application with more than one module. It is designed to modernise the student and visitor experience at Belgium Campus, and it is made up of BC Ways (navigation) and BC Eats (food services).

The problem it aims to address

Navigation and orientation. Large campus layouts and static signage can leave students late for class and first-time visitors confused.

Dining inefficiency. Manual tuckshop orders mean long queues, human error and no real-time view of the menu.

Component

BC Ways: how it works

BC Ways is a two-dimensional digital navigation system built for people on foot. A user picks where they want to go, and the app works out a route and guides them along it.

Searchable campus map

A 2D map showing buildings, offices and services, so people can look up a destination instead of hunting for signs.

Shortest route

Routes are planned with Dijkstra’s shortest-path algorithm over a graph of campus points.

Live guidance

GPS tracking follows the user along the route and recalculates it if they go off track.

Visitor access

Visitors are meant to reach navigation through a QR code, without downloading the app first.

Component

BC Eats: how it works

BC Eats moves the campus tuckshop online. Students view the menu in the app, place an order, and follow its progress, while the tuckshop manages everything from a dashboard.

Digital menu

A menu board that can be updated in real time, so students see what is currently available.

Pre-ordering

Orders are placed from a phone, with cashless payment, and the student chooses collection or delivery.

Order tracking

Orders move through stages (received, preparing, ready, completed) and students are notified as their order changes.

Admin dashboard

Tuckshop staff receive orders and manage the menu from a dashboard that also offers analytics.

Drone delivery is part of the BC Eats plan. It is a proposed option and should be read as planned work, not a finished feature.

Who it is for

The intended value

  • Students: an easier way to find their way around campus and to order food without standing in a queue.
  • First-time visitors: a way to find destinations on campus more easily and with more confidence.
  • Campus staff: a clearer view of tuckshop orders and the menu.

These are the goals set out in our project proposal. We have not yet gathered student survey data to show how well they are met.
