What We Do

Interfaces, systems and smart campus ideas

We’re developing BC Smart Lifestyle to improve the campus experience for students and visitors. By bringing together campus navigation and food ordering, the project aims to make it easier to find places, access services and order food on campus.

Frontend Development

We develop the user interfaces for BC Ways and BC Eats, including the campus map, destination search, food menus and ordering screens. Our focus is on making the app easy to navigate and use.

Backend Development

We work on the application’s data, APIs and system logic to support features such as campus navigation, food ordering and order management.

Smart Campus Solutions

BC Ways helps students and visitors find destinations around campus, while BC Eats supports digital food menus, ordering and collection or delivery options.
