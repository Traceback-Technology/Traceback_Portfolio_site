About Us

Who we are and what we work on

Traceback-Technology is a team of student developers building software together.

Our approach

We work on software development, with some of us focusing on frontend, some on backend, and some across both. The frontend is what people see and use. The backend is the part that makes it all work behind the scenes.

We are also interested in how technology can connect campus activities and services in one place, so people do not have to jump between different things to get what they need.

Right now, that interest is shaped by our current project, the BC Smart Ecosystem.
