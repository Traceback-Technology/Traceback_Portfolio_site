About Us

Who we are and what we work on

Traceback-Technology is a team of student developers building software together.

Our approach

We are developing the BC Smart Ecosystem to improve campus navigation and make food ordering more convenient for students and visitors. The project brings together BC Ways, which helps users find destinations around campus, and BC Eats, which allows users to browse the tuckshop menu and place orders.

The application combines a Flutter frontend with a backend that supports its main features, including navigation, food ordering and order management. By bringing these services together, we aim to make everyday campus activities easier to access in one place.

Right now, that interest is shaped by our current project, the BC Smart Ecosystem.
